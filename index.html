The stitch-up

Here's what actually happened.

Before the election, Albanese said — on the record — he wouldn't touch negative gearing. Wouldn't touch capital gains tax. You believed him. A lot of us did. Some of us bought properties, started businesses, put money into super based on that promise.

Then Budget night came. And they changed everything.

CGT discount? Gone.
Negative gearing? Restricted.
Super tax? Already law.
Trust tax? Coming next.

Nobody voted for this. Nobody was asked. Nobody was told.

You've been stitched up. And the only question left is: who was in on it?
